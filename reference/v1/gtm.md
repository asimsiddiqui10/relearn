## Why Your Product Is Globally Relevant — Not Just India

Every country with a competitive university entrance exam has this market. The pattern is identical everywhere:

- Students buy PDFs, notes, past papers from their coaching institute
- Teachers create proprietary study material
- Students need to query across this material under time pressure
- Citations matter because exam answers need sources
- The institute's competitive advantage is the quality of their material

Your pipeline — document ingestion, grounded Q&A with citations, section summaries, visualizer anchors — is exactly what every exam-prep institute in every country needs. The content changes. The product requirement does not.

## The Sequencing Recommendation

**Year 1:** India only. 50–100 institutes. Prove the model, build case studies, refine Hindi/regional language support.
**Year 2:** Middle East and Bangladesh/Pakistan. Same product, minimal localization, same sales motion, 3x the revenue per student.
**Year 3:** South Korea and Japan. Requires Korean/Japanese UI and localization investment but unlocks 4–5x higher per-student pricing.
**Year 4+:** US/UK. Requires compliance, SOC2, longer enterprise sales but the revenue per institute is 10x India.

The India-first strategy is right not because India is the only market — it's because India gives you the fastest path to proof of concept with the lowest sales friction. The global market is waiting behind it.
